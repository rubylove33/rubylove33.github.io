Frequently Asked Questions

The same questions and answers you'll find inside the app's Help screen — collected here so you can read them before installing, or share a link with a friend.

How should I use the app?
1. Set Goal 2. Walk with Timer or Record Manually 3. Check Stats 4. Manage Photos Try in this order!
How is the daily goal set?
It is automatically updated daily based on your Basic Routine and Custom Schedule in the Goals tab.
Can I see the first-time guide again?
Yes. Go to Settings > Quick Start Guide > Show Home Start Guide Card to show it again on Home. You can choose the language too (current app language / Korean / English).
I tapped Ad Personalization Consent but nothing appeared. Is it an error?
Not always. In some regions or statuses, the CMP privacy options dialog may not be required and may not appear. In that case, the app shows a message explaining it.
Will my records disappear when I turn on sample data?
No. Your records, goals, and reminder settings are safely preserved. Sample data is shown in a separate temporary workspace and your original data is restored when sample mode is turned off.
Do changes I make in sample mode affect my real records?
No. Records added or edited in sample mode are only for preview and are discarded when sample mode is turned off. They do not mix with your personal records.
Do goal or reminder changes in sample mode affect my real settings?
No. Goal changes (Basic Routine / Custom Schedule) and reminder changes in sample mode are temporary and revert when sample mode is turned off.
How is the streak counted?
Rest days (0 min goal) count as achieved. You must complete your daily goal to keep the streak alive.
When can I add photos to the album?
You can add photos only when there is at least 1 second of workout record for that date.
Are scenery and body-change photos managed separately?
All photos are now managed in one album. You can apply templates and create GIFs in the same flow regardless of photo type.
Are templates paid?
All templates are free. Pick any design — Simple, Polaroid, Poster, Magazine, Film, Minimal, Retro, SNS, Dino, or Quote — and use it however you like.
How do I create a GIF?
Collect 3 or more photos in the Photos album to create a timelapse GIF.
How are Walk Clip videos made?
Capture 2 seconds at the start and end of your walk — the two moments are stitched into a single short video saved to Photos. Skipping the start clip means that walk runs without a video. If you capture the start but skip the finish, no video is made (the start clip is auto-cleaned). There is no mid-walk capture option — Walk Clips are about recording the start and the end of your walk.
Can I switch cameras while recording?
Camera switching is available only before recording starts (during the countdown). Tap the camera icon at the top-right of the capture screen. During recording, the toggle is temporarily disabled so the clip stays consistent. You can pick a different camera for the start clip and the finish clip independently.
Why is there a face warning?
Walk Habit prioritizes your privacy. We warn you about sharing photos with faces to protect personal information. We recommend verifying with landscape or object photos, but you can save them if you wish.
I want to reset my data.
You can reset your data in Settings > Reset Data.
